How this page works and what it leaves out
Source
The ICE List (theicelist.org) incident pages for Washington, searched town by town, plus news reports found by web search. The ICE List gathers StopICE community alerts, eyewitness reports, court records and news. Legal-help links are on iceinmyarea.org/resources.
Score
Each report counts 1 on the day it happened and half that 90 days later, a quarter after 180 days, and so on. A town's score is the sum. It favors places with repeated and recent reports.
Only with evidence
Keeps reports backed by video, a court record, eyewitness detail matched to ICE's FOIA arrest log, or a news report. Drops unconfirmed community alerts and index listings.
Wider area
Tacoma, Burien, Renton, Puyallup, Fife and Tukwila entries come from the site's index. Their pages were not opened, so they have no place or time. One undated Tacoma alert is left out.
Left out
Reports that never reached The ICE List. Arrest totals in ICE's own data, which count people rather than places. Anything about future operations, which no public source here describes.
Updates
The ICE List is checked every hour, day and night, along with a news search, and new reports are added. The clock and "days ago" figures use the current time. Scores and the 90-day window count back from the most recent check.
